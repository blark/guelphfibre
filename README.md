# guelphfibre.ca

A public record of three questions put to every candidate for Mayor and for
Ward 2 Council in the City of Guelph's 26 October 2026 municipal election,
and the answers received.

Every candidate is asked the same three questions, in the same words, by
email. Answers are published verbatim and unedited. Candidates who do not
reply are listed as such.

The git history of this repository is the audit trail: each answer is
committed on the day it arrives, so the record cannot be quietly revised
after the fact.

## The questions

1. Will you fund a dig-once policy, so City conduit goes into the ground
   whenever a street is rebuilt, and is offered to any carrier?
2. Will you direct staff to report within six months on the City's spare
   conduit and dark fibre, and the terms on which a competing provider
   could use it?
3. Will you meet with carriers in your first year, with that conduit and
   fibre as the offer, and report publicly on what they tell you?

## Corrections

If you are a candidate and your answer is recorded incorrectly, or you
replied and are shown as not having replied, use the contact link on
guelphfibre.ca and it will be corrected.

Maintained by Mark Baseggio, a resident of Ward 2.
