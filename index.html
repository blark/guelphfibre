Bell announced fibre for 44,000 Guelph homes in April 2022. After the CRTC ordered it to sell competitors access to that fibre at a regulated price, Bell cut more than $1.2 billion from its buildout and stopped. Parts of Guelph were finished. Others were not. The slower address above is the one closer to downtown.

The quotes

In both, the headline figure is a two-year promotional rate. Bell's list price is the smaller line at the foot of each card: Price without limited-time credits: $85/mo.

The questions

Every candidate receives the same three questions, in the same words, by email.

  1. Will you fund a dig-once policy, so City conduit goes into the ground whenever a street is rebuilt, and is offered to any carrier?

  2. Will you direct staff to report within six months on the City's spare conduit and dark fibre, and the terms on which a competing provider could use it?

  3. Will you meet with carriers in your first year, with that conduit and fibre as the offer, and report publicly on what they tell you?

Method

Every candidate is emailed the same three questions in the same words. Answers are reproduced verbatim and in full, without editing or commentary. Candidates who do not reply are recorded as not having replied.

This page is kept in a public git repository. Each answer is committed on the day it arrives, so the history shows when every entry was added and that nothing was revised afterwards.

If you are a candidate and your answer is recorded incorrectly, or you replied and are shown as not having replied, get in touch and it will be corrected.
