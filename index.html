Guelph's two-tier internet

Three questions put to every candidate for Mayor and Ward 2 Council in the 26 October 2026 municipal election. Answers are published verbatim.

Last updated 3 September 2026

Why

Bell sells two different products in Guelph at the same list price. These quotes were taken from bell.ca on the same day, on the same two-year term.

A residential address 2.7 km from City Hall
50 / 10 Mbps
$85/month list
19 Elmira Road · 4.4 km from City Hall
3,000 / 3,000 Mbps
$85/month list

Sixty times the download speed and three hundred times the upload speed, for the same money. The slower address is the one closer to downtown.

Bell announced fibre for 44,000 Guelph homes in April 2022. After the CRTC ordered it to sell competitors access to that fibre at a regulated price, Bell cut more than $1.2 billion from its buildout and stopped. Parts of Guelph were finished. Others were not.

The questions

Every candidate receives the same three questions, in the same words, by email.

  1. Will you fund a dig-once policy, so City conduit goes into the ground whenever a street is rebuilt, and is offered to any carrier?

  2. Will you direct staff to report within six months on the City's spare conduit and dark fibre, and the terms on which a competing provider could use it?

  3. Will you meet with carriers in your first year, with that conduit and fibre as the offer, and report publicly on what they tell you?

Method

Every candidate is emailed the same three questions in the same words. Answers are reproduced verbatim, in full, without editing or commentary. Candidates who do not reply are recorded as not having replied.

This page is maintained in a public git repository. Each answer is committed on the day it arrives, so the history shows when every entry was added and that nothing was revised afterwards.

If you are a candidate and your answer is recorded incorrectly, or you replied and are shown as not having replied, get in touch and it will be corrected.
