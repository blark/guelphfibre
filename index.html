Why this is a council issue

Rural speed, downtown price

50/10 is the bare minimum Ottawa says every farmhouse in Canada should get. It’s the best Bell will sell you downtown, and it costs the same as 3,000 Mbps four kilometres away.

One game in town

Downtown, Rogers cable is the only real option. Where Bell fibre exists, the CRTC ruling also lets competitors sell on it. Without it, there is no competition and no pressure on anyone’s price.

Our streets, their terms

Every cable runs under public roads and past public poles. Bell and Rogers decide who gets wired and what it costs; the City that owns the ground gets no say. It could. That’s what the three questions are about.

How Guelph got here

Bell was never asked to give anything away. It was told to sell competitors access to its fibre at a regulated price, for which it would be paid. It chose to stop building instead.

ShowHide the timeline, 2022–2025

  1. Apr 2022

    Bell promises fibre to 44,000 Guelph homes “by 2024”, “fully funded by Bell.” The Mayor calls it “great news for Guelph.” Announcement

  2. Nov 2023

    The CRTC rules that Bell must sell competitors access to its fibre, at a regulated price. Bell responds by cutting $1 billion from its own build and warning it will “re-consider pending builds in all communities where it had planned to expand.” Bell’s statement

  3. 2024

    Bell’s own deadline passes. Some Guelph streets have fibre. Others don’t.

  4. Jun 2025

    Bell’s cuts reach $1.2 billion, by its own count affecting “more than 1 million homes and businesses in more than 150 communities.” It will not say which. Bell’s statement

  5. Aug 2025

    Ottawa reviews the ruling and lets it stand. Bell has not come back.

Three questions for every candidate

  1. 1

    Will you fund a dig-once policy, so City conduit goes into the ground whenever a street is rebuilt, and is offered to any carrier?

  2. 2

    Will you direct staff to report within six months on the City’s spare conduit and dark fibre, and the terms on which a competing provider could use it?

  3. 3

    Will you meet with carriers in your first year, with that conduit and fibre as the offer, and report publicly on what they tell you?
